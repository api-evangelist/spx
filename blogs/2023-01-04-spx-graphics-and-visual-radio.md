Yle's Swedish language radio station X3M held its annual Red Nose Day (Nenäpäivä) charity broadcast again in November 2022. The broadcast lasted for 66 hours and 6 minutes and it set a new record for the longest radio broadcast in Finland.This year the channel wanted to focus more on the live video stream of the event, so the stream ran for the entire duration of the radio broadcast.
