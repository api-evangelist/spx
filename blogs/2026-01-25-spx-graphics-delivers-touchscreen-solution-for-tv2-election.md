SPX Graphics built TV2 Denmark’s HTML-based touchscreen system for the 2025 elections, integrating live data and video and supporting both pre-election and election-night coverage. The collaboration also showcases the increasing industry adoption of HTML graphics and both organizations’ influence on the EBU’s OGraf standard. A full case study is coming soon.
